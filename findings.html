COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT PROGRAM

Key Findings

Findings from the digital services training program.

1. Improved Digital Awareness

Participants gained better awareness of DigiLocker, Aadhaar and PAN services after the training.

2. Better Understanding

The training helped participants understand how digital government services can be used.

3. Increased Confidence

Practical demonstrations helped participants become more confident while using digital services.

4. Digital Safety Awareness

Participants learned the importance of protecting passwords, OTPs and personal information.

5. Reduced Digital Difficulties

Simple explanations and practical guidance helped reduce confusion about digital services.

6. Positive Response

Participants showed positive interest in using digital government services in future.
