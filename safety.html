DIGITAL AWARENESS

Digital Safety

Learn how to stay safe while using online government services.

Important Safety Practices

🔐 Protect Your Password

Use strong passwords and never share your passwords with other people.

📱 Never Share OTP

Never share an OTP with unknown people over phone calls or messages.

🌐 Use Official Websites

Always check that you are using the correct official website.

⚠️ Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown or suspicious links received through messages or email.

🛡️ Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information with unknown people.

📲 Verify Before You Act

Verify messages, websites and requests before providing any information.
