Training Methodology

Learn how the DigiLocker, Aadhaar and PAN training program is conducted.

Training Process

1. Planning

Prepare the training material and plan the activities for the program.

2. Awareness Session

Introduce participants to DigiLocker, Aadhaar and PAN services.

3. Demonstration

Explain digital services through simple practical demonstrations.

4. Hands-on Training

Help participants understand how digital services can be used.

5. Digital Safety

Explain important safety practices while using online services.

6. Feedback

Collect feedback from participants to understand the impact of training.
